## Stop/narrow audit after interview 30

If 30 qualified interviews yield no urgent workflow access or willingness to
pay, stop broad customer-specific engineering. Record the evidence and choose
one explicit response: narrow the workflow, reposition the product, integrate
with an incumbent, or stop.
